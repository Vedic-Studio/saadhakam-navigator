# YouTube Script Briefs: Yoga, Kriya, Kundalini, Tantra

**Format Guidelines:** 
- Myth-busting approach. Highly visual. 
- Emphasize the historical / traditional roots via ancient art and diagrams vs the modern, commercialized versions.
- Tone is protective, slightly warning, but deeply respectful of the underlying science.

---

### Video 1: The Dark Side of Kundalini Awakening (That Nobody Talks About)
**Target Keyword:** is kundalini awakening real or psychosis, kundalini awakening symptoms physical
**Vibe:** Investigative, slightly intense, highly responsible.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):** 
*(Visual: Rapid cuts of TikToks of people convulsing or breathing erratically, cut suddenly to a quiet shot of a historical drawing of the chakra system.)*
"Search 'Kundalini Awakening' online and you'll find thousands of people trying to force a dormant, evolutionary energy up their spine using intense breathwork. What they aren't telling you is that if you do this before your nervous system is prepared, you aren't going to get enlightened. You might just end up in the psychiatric ward."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- The metaphor: You are running 10,000 volts of electricity through wires designed for 100 volts. The wires melt.
- Discuss the overlap between "spiritual emergency" and "psychosis."
- The signs of a genuine, safe awakening: Increased empathy, clarity, non-attachment. The signs of danger: Paranoia, god-complex, severe bodily tremors, insomnia.
- The take-away: Stop trying to "hack" it. Build the foundation first.

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- An animation of a thin electrical wire glowing, sparking, and breaking to illustrate unprepared nervous systems.
- Use comparative graphics: Text overlapping two columns: "Spiritual Emergency" (eventually integrates) vs "Psychosis" (requires clinical intervention).

### Video 2: Tantra is Not What You Think It Is
**Target Keyword:** what is tantra really about
**Vibe:** Intellectual, eye-opening, historical.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):**
*(Visual: Host standing in front of a whiteboard or holding a book titled "Modern Tantra" featuring a couple in an embrace. The host tosses the book off-screen.)*
"If I say the word 'Tantra,' 99% of people in the West immediately think of bedroom gymnastics. It is arguably the most misunderstood spiritual word in the English language. Classical Indian Tantra has almost nothing to do with sexuality, and everything to do with viewing the universe as a grid of divine energy."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Break down the word: "Tan" (to weave/expand) and "Tra" (tool/instrument). It is a technology of expansion.
- The shift from Vedanta (The world is illusion) to Tantra (The world is real, the world is Shakti / Divine energy).
- Explain the tools of actual Tantra: Mantras (sound codes), Yantras (geometric maps), and intense ritual discipline.

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- Visual contrast: A blurry, neon, modern "Tantra workshop" ad contrasted with a razor-sharp, ancient Sri Yantra mandala. 
- Fast-paced animation showing a loom weaving fabric, visualizing the definition of the word "Tantra."

### Video 3: 5 Red Flags in Your Yoga Studio
**Target Keyword:** red flags in yoga studios, difference between kriya yoga and regular yoga
**Vibe:** Protective, straightforward, myth-busting.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):**
*(Visual: Host rolling out a yoga mat, sitting down, and looking at the camera.)*
"Yoga was designed to dismantle your ego, not inflate someone else's. But as yoga has become a billion-dollar global industry, the line between a genuine spiritual environment and a toxic, cult-like hierarchy has blurred. Here are five massive red flags that mean you need to grab your mat and walk out."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Red Flag 1: The "Guru" Teacher. (They demand obedience in a fitness class).
- Red Flag 2: Pushing past sharp pain. (Ahimsa/Non-violence applies to your own knees).
- Red Flag 3: Teaching advanced Pranayama on day one. (Breathwork alters neurochemistry).
- Differentiate between modern physical Asana and traditional Kriya/Raja yoga (which is mostly internal).

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- Text call-outs mimicking a checklist.
- Subtle, humorous B-roll of stereotypical "red flag" behaviors (e.g., an instructor giving unnecessary adjustments, or talking in a fake whisper-voice).
