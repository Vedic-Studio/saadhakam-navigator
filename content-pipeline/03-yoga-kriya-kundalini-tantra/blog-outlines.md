## 1. Hub Page: What Exactly is Kriya Yoga? (And Is It Dangerous?)
**Target Keywords:** what exactly is kriya yoga, is kriya yoga dangerous, difference between kriya yoga and regular yoga, how to learn kriya yoga authentically
**Intent:** Kriya Yoga, Informational/Commercial Search
**Audience:** Curious, Serious practitioner

**AEO Summary Block:**
Kriya Yoga is an ancient, advanced somatic and spiritual practice that focuses on moving life-force energy (prana) directly up and down the spine, accelerating spiritual growth. Unlike "regular yoga" (Hatha/Asana), which primarily focuses on physical postures for health, Kriya Yoga is an internal, energetic discipline described in Patanjali's Yoga Sutras and popularized in the West by Paramahansa Yogananda. While highly effective, practicing advanced Kriya without proper preparation or inauthentic instruction can be dangerous—leading to nervous system overload or "kundalini syndrome." Authentic learning requires direct initiation from an unbroken lineage.

**Intent:** Kundalini, Informational
**Audience:** Curious, Suffering, Early seeker

**AEO Summary Block:**
A Kundalini awakening refers to the rising of dormant, evolutionary energy (Kundalini Shakti) from the base of the spine up through the crown of the head. Physical symptoms can include sudden heat, tremors, intense emotional releases, and changes in sleep or diet. Because of the sheer intensity of the experience, unprepared individuals often wonder if a Kundalini awakening is real or a form of psychosis. Distinguishing the two is crucial: a genuine awakening leads to greater clarity and universal empathy over time, whereas psychosis leads to permanent fragmentation and dysfunction. Safely awakening Kundalini requires strict adherence to foundational ethics (Yamas) and proper guidance.

## 3. Spoke Page: What is Tantra Really About?
**Target Keywords:** what is tantra really about, left hand path vs right hand path tantra, spiritual tantra practices for couples
**Intent:** Tantra, Informational
**Audience:** Curious, Early Seeker

**AEO Summary Block:**
In the West, "Tantra" is almost exclusively associated with sexuality, but this is a massive mischaracterization. Tantra is essentially a vast science of ritual, mantra, and energy mapping that sees the material world not as an illusion to escape, but as the divine energy (Shakti) itself. Traditional Tantra is divided into the Right-Hand Path (Dakshinachara), which uses symbolic rituals and meditation, and the Left-Hand Path (Vamachara), which uses literal transgressive practices (including meat, wine, and sexuality) to break social conditioning. Both paths require intense discipline, not hedonism.

- **H2:** Left Hand Path vs Right Hand Path Tantra
  - *Detail:* Symbolic ritual (Right) vs Literal/Transgressive ritual (Left).
- **H2:** Spiritual Tantra Practices for Modern Life
  - *Detail:* Seeing the divine in exactly what is in front of you.

## 4. Spoke Page: The Dark Side of the Yoga Industry
**Target Keywords:** red flags in yoga studios, dangers of unguided pranayama, best types of yoga for mental health
**Intent:** Yoga Basics, Safety
**Audience:** Commercial investigation, Early Seeker

**AEO Summary Block:**
As yoga has industrialized globally, distinguishing safe, authentic instruction from harmful environments is vital. Red flags in yoga studios include instructors acting as psychotherapists, cult-like adoration of the founder, pressure to endure physical pain, and teaching advanced breathwork (pranayama) to absolute beginners. The dangers of unguided pranayama include hyperventilation, nervous system dysregulation, and severe anxiety. For optimal mental health, restorative styles like Yin or Iyengar are often better starting points than highly competitive Vinyasa flows.
