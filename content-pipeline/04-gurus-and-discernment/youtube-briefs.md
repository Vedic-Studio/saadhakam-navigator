# YouTube Script Briefs: Gurus and Discernment

**Format Guidelines:** 
- Highly objective, investigative tone. Avoid smear campaigns, but do not shy away from the truth.
- Use the "Tension Test" heavily. Present the strongest possible arguments for BOTH sides before deriving the conclusion.
- Visually clean, organized, documentary-style B-roll.

---

### Video 1: 6 Signs Your Spiritual Guru is Actually a Narcissist
**Target Keyword:** how to spot a fake spiritual guru, red flags in spiritual teachers
**Vibe:** Investigative, urgent, educational.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):** 
*(Visual: Host standing in front of a corkboard with pictures of various disgraced cult leaders, connected by red string).*
"If you walk into a spiritual community and it feels like the air is too thin, and everyone is smiling a little too hard, pay attention. The wellness and yoga industries are currently experiencing an epidemic of charismatic narcissists who have learned the language of enlightenment. Before you hand over your time, your money, or your autonomy, run them through these 6 tests."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Sign 1: Lack of Lineage. (They claim they received their knowledge directly from God/Space, so no one can fact-check them).
- Sign 2: Isolation. (They insist their path is the *only* path, and outside information is 'low vibration').
- Sign 3: Secret Knowledge. (They charge $10,000 for the 'advanced' level that will finally unlock your potential).
- Sign 4: Boundary Erosion. (Financial or sexual crossing under the guise of 'spiritual testing').
- The real test of an enlightened master: Do they actively point you back to your own inner authority?

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- Red warning signs stamped over classic cult-behavior scenarios (reenactments or stock footage).
- A checklist graphic building on the side of the screen as each point is made.

### Video 2: Is Sadhguru (Isha Foundation) Actually a Cult?
**Target Keyword:** is Isha Foundation a cult, Sadhguru criticism explained
**Vibe:** Neutral, measured, analytical.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):**
*(Visual: Rapid montage of Sadhguru meeting world leaders, driving motorcycles, mixed with screenshots of Reddit threads calling him a fraud).*
"He’s spoken at the UN, drives a Ducati, and millions of people claim his practices cured their illnesses. But if you look online, thousands of critics call him a brilliant marketer running a massive cult. The truth about Sadhguru and the Isha Foundation isn't black and white. Let's look at the actual evidence—both the controversies and the peer-reviewed science behind his methods."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Present the Criticisms: The aggressive marketing funnels, the massive volunteer labor force, political ties, and the lack of a named guru in his own past.
- Present the Defenses: The massive ecological work (Save Soil), the millions of people who report genuine life changes.
- The nuance: You can absolutely utilize the technology he teaches (Shambhavi Mahamudra) without adopting the dogma or worshipping the man. Extract the tool, leave the personality.

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- Two-column graphics: "The Criticisms" vs "The Defenses."
- Objective, documentary-style zooming on newspaper headlines and foundation websites. No spooky music—keep it entirely neutral and factual.

### Video 3: The Most Powerful Meditation You Aren't Doing (Who Am I?)
**Target Keyword:** Ramana Maharshi who am i technique
**Vibe:** Quiet, profound, laser-focused.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):**
*(Visual: Extreme close-up of a still pond. A single drop hits it. The ripples expand. The camera zooms into the center of the ripple).*
"What if every single problem you have right now is based on a case of mistaken identity? A hundred years ago, a teenager in India realized that all suffering comes from attaching the word 'I' to the wrong thing. He developed a technique that bypasses the intellect completely. It’s called Self-Inquiry. And it requires asking only one question."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Brief intro to Ramana Maharshi (The sage of Arunachala).
- Explain the technique: When a depressing thought arises, ask "Who is having this thought?" The answer is "Me." Then ask, "Who am I?"
- The goal is not to answer the question verbally! It is to turn the flashlight of attention back onto the battery itself. To look at the observer.
- How this leads to the realization of pure awareness (the core of Advaita Vedanta).

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- Historical photos of Sri Ramana Maharshi.
- Visual animation of a camera pointing at a subject, and then a mirror turning the camera's lens back onto itself.
