# YouTube Script Briefs: Spiritual Tourism India

**Format Guidelines:** 
- Heavy emphasis on high-quality, authentic B-roll (Varanasi ghats, Himalayan foothills). Not generic stock footage.
- Tone should be an experienced traveler talking to a naive beginner. Protective, realistic, slightly gritty.
- No "spiritual bypassing" / over-romaticizing.

---

### Video 1: Do Not Go to an Indian Ashram Before Knowing This
**Target Keyword:** etiquette rules for visiting Hindu temples, what to pack for an Indian ashram
**Vibe:** Direct, culturally protective, instructional.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):** 
*(Visual: Host packing a bag, throwing out expensive athletic wear and replacing it with plain cotton clothes.)*
"Every year, thousands of Westerners fly to India looking for spiritual enlightenment, and immediately offend the local monks on day one. An ashram is not a resort, and it is not a gym. If you want access to the real teachings, you need to follow their rules. Here are the 5 unspoken laws of Indian ashram etiquette."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- The Dress Code: Shoulders and knees covered. Baggy is better. Do not wear Lululemon to the temple.
- The Right Hand Rule: Never point the bottom of your feet at a teacher or an altar. Never eat or hand things to people with your left hand.
- The Mindset: You are there to serve and observe, not to "consume" spirituality like a tourist attraction.
- Packing List: Why you need slip-on shoes (you will take them off 20 times a day) and electrolytes.

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- Clear visual "Do and Don't" demonstrations (e.g., foot placement while sitting).
- POV shots of walking through a crowded temple, taking shoes off, accepting food with the right hand.

### Video 2: Rishikesh vs Dharamshala (Where Should You Go?)
**Target Keyword:** Rishikesh vs Dharamshala for spirituality, best ashrams in Rishikesh for westerners
**Vibe:** Informational, comparative, scenic.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):**
*(Visual: Split screen. Left side: The rushing Ganges and fire rituals in Rishikesh. Right side: Snowy peaks and Tibetan prayer wheels in Dharamshala).*
"If you are planning a spiritual journey to the foothills of the Himalayas, you have two legendary choices. To the south, Rishikesh: the loud, ancient, Hindu capital of Yoga. To the north, Dharamshala: the quiet, crisp, Tibetan Buddhist home of the Dalai Lama. They offer completely different definitions of enlightenment. Let's figure out which one is right for you."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Compare the philosophies: Hindu/Vedanta (The self is everything) vs Buddhist (There is no self). 
- Compare the practices: Asana, Kriya, and chanting (Rishikesh) vs Vipassana, silence, and analytical meditation (Dharamshala).
- Logistics: Recommend specific ashrams for both styles. Discuss pricing for 200-hour YTTs vs drop-in retreats.

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- High-quality comparison shots. Match cuts between a Hindu Aarti ceremony on the Ganges and Tibetan Monks debating in McLeod Ganj.
- Map graphics showing altitude and location differences.

### Video 3: I Survived 10 Days of Complete Silence (Vipassana in India)
**Target Keyword:** 10 day vipassana meditation retreat India, silent meditation retreats in India
**Vibe:** Intense, honest, transformative.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):**
*(Visual: Host looking into the camera. No background music. Complete silence for 3 seconds. Then, sudden fast-forward montage of busy Indian streets cutting back to silence).*
"No talking. No reading. No writing. No phones. Not even making eye contact with another human being for ten days straight. And to do it, you sit cross-legged for 10 hours a day while your whole body aches. A 10-day Vipassana retreat in India is not a vacation. It is psychological surgery without anesthesia. Here is exactly what happens to your brain when you try it."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Break down the timeline: The boredom of Day 1 and 2. The agony of Day 3 when the ego rebels. The breakthrough of Day 6.
- The actual technique: You aren't chanting mantras. You are just scanning the body for sensations and training the mind to stop reacting to the pain. You are rewiring the habit of suffering.
- The logistics: It is completely free (donation-based). S.N. Goenka centers.

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- Recreations or slow-motion B-roll of staring at a blank wall, watching the clock.
- Visual animations of neuroplasticity (brain connections rewiring as a reaction to physical sensation is bypassed).
