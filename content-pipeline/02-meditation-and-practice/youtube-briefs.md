# YouTube Script Briefs: Meditation & Practice

**Format Guidelines:** 
- Focus on practical delivery. These are people in pain (anxiety, burnout). 
- Speak slowly. Use calming, low-contrast b-roll. 
- Avoid toxic positivity. Acknowledge the suffering immediately.

---

### Video 1: Do THIS if Meditation Makes Your Anxiety Worse
**Target Keyword:** meditation for severe anxiety, guided meditation to stop overthinking
**Vibe:** Grounding, clinical yet empathetic, highly practical.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):** 
*(Visual: Host looking directly at camera. Text on screen: "Does meditating make you panic?")*
"If you have severe anxiety, and someone tells you to just 'sit quietly and clear your mind'—they are giving you terrible advice. When your nervous system is trapped in fight-or-flight, sitting in a silent room can actually trigger a panic attack. Today, I'm going to show you the 'override switch' for your nervous system."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Validate the viewer: It's normal to feel worse during silent meditation if you have anxiety or trauma.
- The Science: Explain the sympathetic (gas pedal) vs parasympathetic (brakes) nervous system.
- The Solution: Active meditation. Give the mind a bone to chew on. Teach the 4-7-8 breathing technique, or a simple physical grounding exercise (5 things you can see, 4 you can touch).

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- Animated diagram of the vagus nerve connecting the brain to the gut, showing how breath directly signals the brain that it is safe.
- Demonstrative B-roll of the host actually doing the breathing exercise, allowing the viewer to pace their breath to the video.

### Video 2: The Spiritual Cure for Corporate Burnout
**Target Keyword:** meditation for corporate burnout, spiritual practices for exhaustion
**Vibe:** Direct, reality-checking, deeply freeing.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):**
*(Visual: Fast-forward footage of a busy office, subway commutes, endless emails pouring into an inbox, ending on a shot of someone staring blankly at a laptop at 11 PM.)*
"You cannot cure corporate burnout with a two-week vacation. Because burnout isn't caused by working too many hours. It's caused by friction. It's caused by caring too much about things you can't control. Thousands of years ago, the Bhagavad Gita proposed a radical solution to this exact kind of exhaustion. It's called Karma Yoga."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Define the modern problem: Deriving our entire self-worth from external metrics (promotions, slack messages, KPI's).
- Introduce Karma Yoga: "You have a right to the work, but never to the fruits of the work."
- How to practice detachment in the office: Care deeply about the quality of the spreadsheet you are making, but detach completely from what your boss says about it.

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- Visual contrast: frantic corporate environments cut against calm, slow-moving nature shots or ancient temple carvings representing duty.
- Text graphics on screen crossing out "Work for Reward" and replacing it with "Work as Offering."

### Video 3: How to Meditate if You Can't Sit Still (Vedic Meditation)
**Target Keyword:** mindfulness meditation vs vedic meditation
**Vibe:** Educational, relieving, accessible.

**The Hook (0:00 - 0:15):**
*(Visual: Host sitting cross-legged, visibly frustrated, opening one eye to look at a ticking clock. BZZT sound effect.)*
"If you've tried to meditate by focusing on your breath, but your brain keeps screaming about your grocery list and unpaid taxes—you aren't failing. You're just using a technique that is really hard for modern, over-stimulated brains. Let me show you an ancient Indian alternative that requires zero concentration."

**A-Roll/Script Core Points:**
- Contrast Mindfulness (Vipassana - observing thoughts/breath) with Vedic/Japa Meditation (using a mantra).
- The "Monkey Mind" analogy: If you tell a monkey to sit still, it will terrorize you. If you give the monkey a pole to slide up and down, it focuses. The mantra is the pole.
- Explain that the sound of the mantra physically vibrates the palate and calms the system, without forcing you to fight your thoughts.

**B-roll & Visual Directions:**
- An animated monkey wreaking havoc in a room (representing thoughts), then sitting peacefully sliding up and down a pole (the mantra).
- Simple graphics showing the brain waves shifting from Beta (waking) to Alpha/Theta (deep rest) as a mantra is repeated.
