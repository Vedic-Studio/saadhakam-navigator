# Sadhaka X Content Strategy
**Goal:** Drive bookmarks, article reads, and conversions (newsletter, app, Gumroad PDFs) from X
**Version:** v1 | March 2026

---

## 1. Strategic Frame

Sadhaka is the *trusted guide for the Western seeker* — not a brand pushing product, but the person in the room who actually understands both Indian traditions AND modern psychology, and can translate between them.

On X, this translates to: **be the account people screenshot, bookmark, and share with one friend who needs this.**

Conversion is downstream of trust. The content job is to create "I need to follow this account" moments repeatedly. Clicks on links, newsletter signups, and Gumroad buys happen *after* someone has seen 3-5 pieces that made them feel understood.

## 3. Content Pillars (What to Post)

### Pillar 1: The Explainer
**Purpose:** Bookmark magnet. Establish authority.
**Format:** Thread (8-15 tweets) or long-form single post
**Formula:** Common misconception → what the tradition actually says → why it matters for modern life → CTA

Examples:
- "Most people think karma means what goes around comes around. The actual teaching is more interesting."
- "What Westerners call 'mindfulness' is a fragment of a much bigger system. Here's the full map."
- "Dharma isn't your 'purpose'. Here's what it actually means — and why the difference matters."

**Bookmark trigger:** Completeness. The reader thinks "I'll want this again."

---

### Pillar 2: The Reframe
**Purpose:** Virality + profile visits. Disagreement-bait (good kind).
**Format:** Single punchy tweet or short thread (3-5 tweets)
**Formula:** Conventional wisdom → challenge it with tradition → drop the implication

Examples:
- "Modern yoga studios have almost nothing to do with yoga. The word 'yoga' in the Gita doesn't mean movement."
- "Sadhguru, Deepak Chopra, and Eckhart Tolle are not the same thing. Here's the difference."
- "The West imported meditation and exported the context that makes it work."
- "Manifestation culture is a distorted version of something real. Here's the original."

**Bookmark trigger:** Saves it to send to someone else. Or to rebut. Both work.

---

### Pillar 3: The Practical Wisdom Drop
**Purpose:** Daily-use content. Drives repeat visits.
**Format:** Single tweet or 3-tweet mini-thread
**Formula:** Ancient concept → modern life application → one-line landing

Examples:
- "The Gita has a framework for decision paralysis that's better than anything in the productivity section. It's called nishkama karma..."
- "Overwhelmed? Indian traditions have a word for the mental noise causing it: vritti. And they have a protocol."
- "Feeling like nothing you do matters: that's not depression. Indian philosophy has a precise diagnosis and it's more useful."

**Bookmark trigger:** Solves a felt problem. Reader saves it for a bad day.

---

### Pillar 4: The Sacred Context
**Purpose:** Differentiation + aspiration. Drive Gumroad/course interest.
**Format:** Visual-led post + thread, or standalone richly-described post
**Formula:** Place/practice/text → why it matters → what it unlocks

Examples:
- "Kailasa Temple was carved from a single rock, top to bottom. It encodes an entire cosmology. Let me show you."
- "The Kumbh Mela is the largest human gathering on Earth. Why do 400M people go? It's not what you think."
- "There are 108 Upanishads. These 3 will change how you think about consciousness."

**Bookmark trigger:** Beauty + depth. Reference material.

---

### Pillar 5: The Discernment Post
**Purpose:** High-engagement. Builds trust by NOT being promotional.
**Format:** Thread or nuanced single post
**Formula:** Hard question → honest answer → what to look for instead

Examples:
- "Is Sadhguru legit? I'm going to give you the framework to decide for yourself."
- "How to tell a real guru from a brand. 5 signals from the tradition itself."
- "Problems with Indian gurus — and why the tradition predicted them."

**Bookmark trigger:** People save "the honest take" to share when the debate comes up in their group chat.

---

### Pillar 6: The Thread Essay (Weekly Anchor)
**Purpose:** Long-form read. Newsletter/article funnel. Drives link clicks.
**Format:** 10-20 tweet thread. Feels like a standalone essay.
**Structure:**
- Tweet 1: Hook (bold claim or question)
- Tweets 2-3: Why this question matters / what's at stake
- Tweets 4-12: The actual teaching, layered carefully
- Tweet 13: The modern application
- Tweet 14: What to do next (article link, newsletter CTA, Gumroad mention)
- Tweet 15: "Save this / share this" prompt

This is the workhorse post. One per week minimum.

## 5. Hook Bank (First-Tweet Formulas)

These are the sentence types that stop the scroll. Use one per post.

**The Contrarian:**
> "Everything you've heard about [concept] is incomplete. The original teaching is harder — and more useful."

**The Precision:**
> "There's a Sanskrit word for the thing you're feeling right now. And there's been a protocol for it for 3,000 years."

**The Stakes:**
> "If you want to understand modern wellness, you need to understand what it stripped out of Indian philosophy. Thread."

**The Named Problem:**
> "Burnout, midlife crisis, feeling like nothing matters — Indian traditions have very specific names for all of these. They're not pathologies."

**The Visual:**
> "Kailasa Temple is the most ambitious construction project in human history. And almost no one in the West knows what it means. Let me fix that."

**The Honest Take:**
> "I'm going to say something unpopular about Sadhguru. Not what critics say. What the tradition itself would say."

**The Comparison:**
> "Stoicism is having a moment. But Vedanta covers everything Stoicism covers — and goes further. Here's the map."

## 6. Reply Strategy (How Links Get Clicked)

X's algorithm suppresses external links in the main post. The workaround is the **reply drop**.

**Pattern:**
1. Post the thread or single tweet — no link in the post
2. Reply to yourself immediately: "Full article + 3 practices for this → [link]"
3. Or: "I wrote the full version with examples here → [link]"

**DM trigger pattern** (drives Gumroad):
- In the thread ending: "Reply with 'DHARMA' and I'll send you the full PDF breakdown."
- Set up DM automation (Manychat or similar) to send the Gumroad link automatically.
- This creates a conversion touchpoint without leaving X.

**Profile link:**
- Bio link → Linktree or single landing page with: Newsletter signup (primary) + App link + Best Gumroad PDF (secondary)
- Pin the best thread essay. Update monthly.

## 11. Voice and Constraints

**Do:**
- Write at the level of a serious, curious non-expert
- Use Sanskrit terms but always translate immediately
- Take a position — hedging is forgettable
- Cite the tradition ("the Gita says..." / "In Advaita Vedanta...")
- Respect the tradition while making it legible

**Don't:**
- Sound like a yoga studio caption
- Use "journey", "sacred space", "hold space", "energy"
- Moralize or lecture
- Promote without educating first
- Link in main post body (algorithm penalty — use reply drop)
