# X/Twitter Adaptation Rules

## Format Constraints
- Maximum 280 characters per tweet.
- Thread must have 3 to 7 tweets.
- The first tweet is the Hook. It must grab attention, state the value, and tease the thread. No emojis in the first line.
- The final tweet must be a CTA (Call to Action) leading the reader back to the website.
- Use line breaks (empty lines) between ideas within a tweet to make it scannable.

## Voice Constraints
- Match the underlying tone of the article but adapt for social media (snappy, direct, authoritative).
- Remove academic fluff. Translate complex terms directly.

## Structure
- Tweet 1: Hook (The painful problem or missing insight)
- Tweet 2: Definition/Core Idea
- Tweet 3-5: Practical application or insights
- Tweet N: CTA (Read the full guide)
