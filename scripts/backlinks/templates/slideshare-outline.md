### Slide 3: What is Sadhaka?
- Free, open encyclopaedic platform for Hindu philosophical traditions
- Covers Vedanta, Shaiva, Shakta, and Vaishnava schools
- Built for modern seekers, scholars, and the curious
- URL: opensadhaka.com

### Slide 4: Content Pillars
1. **Philosophy** — Advaita, Dvaita, Vishishtadvaita, and more
2. **Sacred Texts** — Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, Stotras
3. **Practices** — Meditation, Yoga, Puja, Mantra
4. **Sanskrit** — 2000+ word vocabulary database
5. **Jyotish** — Vedic astrology encyclopaedia

### Slide 5: Sacred Text Coverage
- **Bhagavad Gita**: All 700 shlokas with Sanskrit, transliteration, and meaning
- **Vishnu Sahasranama**: 1000 names with individual analysis
- **Lalita Sahasranama**: 1000 names with individual analysis
- **Shiva Tandava Stotram**: Verse-by-verse translation flow with literary, mythological, and philosophical analysis

### Slide 6: Unique Features
- **Faith Finder Quiz** — interactive tool to discover your philosophical alignment

- **Sanskrit Database** — searchable vocabulary with etymology and usage
- **Encyclopaedia entries** — 50+ concepts, traditions, texts, and historical figures
