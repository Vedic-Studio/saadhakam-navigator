# Voice Review Pass (Layer 4)

Run AFTER drafting, never during generation. This is a separate editorial pass on the completed draft.

---

## Pass 1: Em-Dash Elimination

Replace every em dash (—) with one of:
- Period + new sentence
- Comma (if the aside is short)
- Colon (if introducing an explanation)
- Parentheses (if truly parenthetical)

Em dashes are the single highest-impact cleanup. Kill them all.

## Pass 2: Adverb & Hedge Kill

Remove or replace:
- Adverbs that weaken: "simply", "merely", "just", "really", "very", "quite", "rather", "somewhat"
- Hedges: "it seems", "one might argue", "it could be said", "perhaps", "in some sense"
- Empty intensifiers: "truly", "deeply", "profoundly", "fundamentally"

Replace with specific claims or delete entirely.

## Pass 3: Stop-Slop Scan

### Banned Phrases (hardban)
journey, navigate, tapestry, landscape, holistic, testament to, at its core, in essence, it is worth noting, delve into, moreover/furthermore (as openers), arguably, notably, living wisdom, unbroken continuity, full breadth, and beyond, not surface spirituality, goes deep, rooted in authentic knowledge

### Banned Structures
- "Not X, but Y" formulas used more than once per article
- Three-item lists with ascending drama ("from A to B to the very C")
- Sentences starting with "This" referring vaguely to the previous paragraph
- Paragraphs that end with a question used as a cliffhanger

### Rhythm Check
- No more than 2 consecutive sentences of the same length
- Vary paragraph length (1-3 sentences)
- Short declarative sentences after complex explanations

## Pass 4: Sadhaka Voice Check

- Does it read like a knowledgeable friend explaining over coffee, not a textbook or a sermon?
- Is every Sanskrit term introduced after the concept is felt in English?
- Are modern bridges used as on-ramps, not equivalences?
- Are source attributions specific (named text, chapter, commentator)?
- Does the tradition feel more sophisticated than the modern frame?

## Quality Score

Rate on 5 dimensions (1-10 each):

1. **Directness** — does every sentence earn its place? No filler, no throat-clearing.
2. **Rhythm** — does the prose have varied cadence? Short punches after long explanations.
3. **Trust** — are claims grounded in named sources? No hand-waving.
4. **Authenticity** — does it sound like a person wrote it? No AI tells.
5. **Density** — is information-per-sentence high? No padding.

**Minimum to publish: 35/50**

Anything below 35 goes back for revision with specific notes on which dimensions failed.
