## People

- [Panini](people/panini.md) — ~5th c. BCE grammarian, author of the Ashtadhyayi
- [Pingala](people/pingala.md) — prosodist, associated with the Chandas Sutra combinatorial tradition
- [Virahanka](people/virahanka.md) — later prosodist credited with the recurrence now known as Fibonacci numbers
- [Hemachandra](people/hemachandra.md) — 12th c. Jain scholar, key link in the Fibonacci attribution chain
- [Halayudha](people/halayudha.md) — 10th c. commentator who preserved the Meru-prastara discussion
- [Lagadha](people/lagadha.md) — traditional author of Vedanga Jyotisha
- [Kanada](people/kanada.md) — founder of Vaisheshika; articulates anu/paramanu atomism
- [Charaka](people/charaka.md) — canonical author-redactor of the Charaka Samhita
- [Kautilya](people/kautilya.md) — attributed author of the Arthashastra; likely a composite tradition
- [R. Shamasastry](people/shamasastry.md) — the librarian who rediscovered the Arthashastra manuscript in 1905
- [Patrick Olivelle](people/olivelle.md) — modern Indologist; major Arthashastra and Dharmashastra translations
- [George Cardona](people/cardona.md) — leading modern Panini scholar
- [Kenneth Zysk](people/zysk.md) — historian of Vedic medicine, "Medicine in the Veda"
- [Dominik Wujastyk](people/wujastyk.md) — historian of Ayurveda, "The Roots of Ayurveda"
- [Kim Plofker](people/plofker.md) — historian of Indian mathematics, "Mathematics in India"
