## Texts

- [Rigveda](texts/rigveda.md) — oldest layer of shruti; source of the Gambler's Lament and women-rishi attributions
- [Atharvaveda](texts/atharvaveda.md) — Vedic healing hymns; religio-medical stratum before classical Ayurveda
- [Vedanga Jyotisha](texts/vedanga-jyotisha.md) — earliest calendrical manual attributed to Lagadha
- [Surya Siddhanta](texts/surya-siddhanta.md) — Siddhanta-era astronomical treatise, multi-recension
- [Chandas Sutra (Pingala)](texts/chandas-sutra.md) — foundational prosody text; source of the combinatorial tradition
- [Ashtadhyayi (Panini)](texts/ashtadhyayi.md) — eight-chapter derivational grammar of Classical Sanskrit
- [Arthashastra (Kautilya)](texts/arthashastra.md) — layered treatise on statecraft, law, economy, and intelligence
- [Charaka Samhita](texts/charaka-samhita.md) — foundational classical Ayurveda compendium
- [Sushruta Samhita](texts/sushruta-samhita.md) — surgical and medical compendium in the Ayurvedic tradition
- [Vaisheshika Sutras (Kanada)](texts/vaisheshika-sutras.md) — foundational atomist metaphysics
- [Nyaya Sutras (Gautama / Akshapada)](texts/nyaya-sutras.md) — foundational logic text; five-membered inference schema
- [Mimamsa Sutras (Jaimini)](texts/mimamsa-sutras.md) — foundational Purva Mimamsa text on Vedic hermeneutics and dharma
- [Samkhya Karika (Ishvarakrishna)](texts/samkhya-karika.md) — earliest surviving systematic Samkhya treatise (c. 4th–5th c. CE)
- [Yoga Sutras (Patanjali)](texts/yoga-sutras.md) — 195 sutras on classical Yoga, ashtanga, samadhi, kaivalya
- [Brahma Sutras (Badarayana)](texts/brahma-sutras.md) — the foundational Vedanta sutras; the single most commented text in Indian philosophy
- [Manusmriti (Manava Dharmashastra)](texts/manusmriti.md) — the most famous dharmashastra; Olivelle's 2005 critical edition
- [Principal Upanishads](texts/principal-upanishads.md) — the ten canonical Upanishads and the scriptural source of Vedanta
- [Aryabhatiya (Aryabhata)](texts/aryabhatiya.md) — 499 CE; pi, sine tables, kuttaka, and the earth-rotation claim
- [Brahmasphutasiddhanta (Brahmagupta)](texts/brahmasphutasiddhanta.md) — 628 CE; the earliest systematic arithmetic of zero and negatives
- [Lilavati (Bhaskara II)](texts/lilavati.md) — 1150 CE; the arithmetic book of the Siddhanta Shiromani; most widely used Indian math textbook
- [Natya Shastra (Bharata Muni)](texts/natya-shastra.md) — foundational text of classical Indian performing arts and rasa aesthetics

## People

- [R. Shamasastry](people/shamasastry.md) — the librarian who rediscovered the Arthashastra manuscript in 1905
- [Patrick Olivelle](people/olivelle.md) — modern Indologist; major Arthashastra and Dharmashastra translations
- [George Cardona](people/cardona.md) — leading modern Panini scholar

- [Kim Plofker](people/plofker.md) — historian of Indian mathematics, "Mathematics in India"
- [Aryabhata](people/aryabhata.md) — 476–550 CE; author of the Aryabhatiya; the first named classical Indian mathematician-astronomer
- [Brahmagupta](people/brahmagupta.md) — 598–668 CE; author of the Brahmasphutasiddhanta; the earliest systematic arithmetic of zero
- [Bhaskara II (Bhaskaracharya)](people/bhaskara-ii.md) — 1114–1185 CE; author of the Siddhanta Shiromani; chakravala method for Pell's equation
- [Madhava of Sangamagrama](people/madhava-sangamagrama.md) — c. 1340–1425 CE; founder of the Kerala school; pre-calculus infinite series
- [Gautama (Akshapada)](people/gautama-akshapada.md) — traditional author of the Nyaya Sutras; the darshana's legendary founder
- [Jaimini](people/jaimini.md) — traditional author of the Mimamsa Sutras; founder of Purva Mimamsa
- [Patanjali (Yoga)](people/patanjali.md) — 4th–5th c. CE; compiler of the Yoga Sutras; distinct from Patanjali the grammarian
- [Bharata Muni](people/bharata-muni.md) — traditional (largely legendary) author of the Natya Shastra
- [Kumarila Bhatta](people/kumarila-bhatta.md) — 7th c. CE; founder of the Bhatta sub-school of Mimamsa; epistemologist and anti-Buddhist polemicist
- [B. K. Matilal](people/matilal.md) — 20th c. Oxford Spalding Professor; made analytic engagement with Indian philosophy mainstream
- [Jonardon Ganeri](people/ganeri.md) — contemporary scholar of classical and early modern Indian philosophy; editor of the Oxford Handbook of Indian Philosophy

- [Dharmayuddha vs Kutayuddha](concepts/dharmayuddha-vs-kutayuddha.md) — just war vs pragmatic war in Indian military ethics
- [Pramana (means of knowledge)](concepts/pramana-epistemology.md) — perception, inference, testimony across the darshanas
- [Darshanas framework](concepts/darshanas-framework.md) — the six orthodox schools as a classificatory map (Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Samkhya, Yoga, Mimamsa, Vedanta)
- [Vedangas framework](concepts/vedangas-framework.md) — the six auxiliary sciences of the Veda (Shiksha, Chandas, Vyakarana, Nirukta, Kalpa, Jyotisha)
- [Upavedas framework](concepts/upavedas-framework.md) — the four applied-knowledge systems (Ayurveda, Dhanurveda, Gandharvaveda, Sthapatyaveda)
- [Aksha Sukta (Gambler's Lament)](concepts/aksha-sukta-addiction.md) — Rigveda 10.34 as the earliest surviving first-person portrait of addiction phenomenology
- [Padarthas](concepts/padarthas.md) — Vaisheshika's six (later seven) ontological categories: dravya, guna, karma, samanya, vishesha, samavaya, abhava
- [Guna theory](concepts/guna-theory.md) — the three gunas of Samkhya-Yoga (sattva, rajas, tamas) as constituents of prakriti, not as qualities of substances
- [Anumana (inference)](concepts/anumana.md) — Nyaya's five-membered inference schema, pervasion (vyapti), and the classical Indian logic tradition
- [Apaurusheyatva](concepts/apaurusheyatva.md) — Mimamsa's doctrine that the Veda has no author (not human, not divine) and is eternal
