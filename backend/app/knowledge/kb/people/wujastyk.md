## One-line identity

A historian of Indian medicine at the University of Alberta whose The Roots of Ayurveda is the most accessible modern scholarly anthology of classical Ayurvedic texts in English.

## Works

- **The Roots of Ayurveda (Penguin Classics, 2003)** — selected translations from the classical Ayurvedic compendia with scholarly introduction and framing
- Journal articles on the history of Ayurveda, manuscripts, and textual history
- Work on the Alchemy and Rasa Shastra tradition

## What they are cited for in this KB

- The best accessible entry point into classical Ayurvedic literature for readers who are not specialists in Sanskrit medical texts
- Careful modern framing of how the classical compendia developed textually
- Honest treatment of the boundary between traditional explanatory frameworks and modern biomedicine

## What they are NOT to be credited with

- A full translation of any single Ayurvedic compendium — The Roots of Ayurveda is an anthology, not a complete rendering of Charaka or Sushruta
- Validating or invalidating traditional medicine through biomedical research; his work is historical and textual, not clinical-trial-based
