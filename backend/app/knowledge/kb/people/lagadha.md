## One-line identity

The traditional author of the [Vedanga Jyotisha](../texts/vedanga-jyotisha.md), the earliest surviving Indian text devoted to calendrical astronomy in service of ritual scheduling.

## Works

- [Vedanga Jyotisha](../texts/vedanga-jyotisha.md) (in both Rk and Yajus recensions by tradition)

## What they are cited for in this KB

- The traditional authorship of the earliest Indian calendrical astronomy manual
- The practical framing of early Indian jyotisha as ritual scheduling rather than planetary modeling

## What they are NOT to be credited with

- Any Siddhanta-phase planetary models — these belong to a much later tradition
- A specific historical identity beyond attribution. "Lagadha" functions in the tradition as the *attributed* author of the Vedanga Jyotisha; biographical detail is scarce and dating is uncertain.

## Sources on this person

- **Plofker, Mathematics in India (2009)** — the best modern entry point, with appropriate scholarly caution about what we actually know about Lagadha
