## One-line identity

The founder of the Vaisheshika darshana and the attributed author of the [Vaisheshika Sutras](../texts/vaisheshika-sutras.md) — the foundational text of classical Indian atomism.

## Works

- [Vaisheshika Sutras](../texts/vaisheshika-sutras.md)

## What they are cited for in this KB

- The articulation of the six padarthas (substance, quality, action, generality, particularity, inherence)
- The atomist theory of composition through anu/paramanu
- A reasoned account of change, composition, and perception grounded in inference from observed composites
- The starting point of the Vaisheshika tradition that eventually fuses with Nyaya into a combined Nyaya-Vaisheshika school

## What they are NOT to be credited with

- **"Discovering atoms before Democritus."** Dating is too uncertain to support clean priority claims; more importantly, both Democritean and Kanada's atomisms are *philosophical* (not experimental) claims about composition.
- **"Discovering modern atomic theory."** The framework explains composition and change inferentially, not through experiment or measurement.
- **A single unified "Indian atomism."** Buddhist, Jaina, and Vaisheshika atomisms are distinct; Kanada represents the Vaisheshika line specifically.
