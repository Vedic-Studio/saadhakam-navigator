## One-line identity

A historian of Indian medicine at the University of Copenhagen whose Medicine in the Veda is the definitive modern scholarly treatment of the religio-medical stratum of the Atharvaveda and related Vedic literature.

## Works

- **Medicine in the Veda (Transactions of the American Philosophical Society; reissued 1998)** — translations and annotations of medical hymns from the Rigveda and Atharvaveda
- **Asceticism and Healing in Ancient India: Medicine in the Buddhist Monastery** — the development of systematic Indian medicine in the monastic context
- Extensive journal articles

## What they are cited for in this KB

- The best modern scholarly frame for Stage 1 of the Indian medical tradition: Vedic healing as religio-medical practice, prior to classical Ayurveda
- The argument that early Indian medicine is a healing tradition *intertwined* with religion, not a separable "proto-biomedicine"
- The role of Buddhist monastic contexts in the development of systematic Indian medicine

## What they are NOT to be credited with

- A full classical Ayurveda translation — for that see [Wujastyk](wujastyk.md)
- Endorsing the "Ayurveda is 5000 years of unchanging science" framing; his work emphasizes historical development and context
