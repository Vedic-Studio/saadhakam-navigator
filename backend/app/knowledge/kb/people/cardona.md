## One-line identity

The leading modern scholar of Panini and the vyakarana tradition, whose Panini: His Work and Its Traditions is the single best reference in English for understanding the Ashtadhyayi and its commentarial apparatus.

## Works

- **Panini: His Work and Its Traditions (Motilal Banarsidass, 2nd ed. 1997)** — the definitive modern study
- **Panini: A Survey of Research (1976)** — earlier survey volume
- Extensive journal articles on Paninian grammar and Sanskrit linguistics

## What they are cited for in this KB

- Comprehensive analysis of the Ashtadhyayi as a derivational system rather than a descriptive grammar
- The structure of rules, meta-rules, and commentarial interpretation in the Paninian tradition
- The bridge between classical Indian grammatical scholarship and modern linguistic analysis

## What they are NOT to be credited with

- The "Panini invented computer science" framing — Cardona's work is careful and historically grounded; the sensationalized framing comes from elsewhere
- A single definitive formalization of Panini in modern computational terms; that is a separate line of work (Kiparsky, Penn, Lowe and others)
