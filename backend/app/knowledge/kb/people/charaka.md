## One-line identity

The traditional redactor whose name anchors the [Charaka Samhita](../texts/charaka-samhita.md) — the foundational internal-medicine compendium of classical Ayurveda.

## Works

- [Charaka Samhita](../texts/charaka-samhita.md) — traditional attribution as the redactor of an earlier text by Agnivesha; later redaction attributed to Dridhabala

## What they are cited for in this KB

- The foundational internal-medicine synthesis of classical Ayurveda
- The systematic doctrinal presentation of the tridosha framework in the context of diagnosis, treatment, and clinical reasoning
- The Vimanasthana section on clinical epistemology — how a physician should reason about evidence in the clinic

## What they are NOT to be credited with

- Writing every word of the received Charaka Samhita. The traditional account is clear that Charaka is a *redactor* of an earlier tradition, and further redaction was done by Dridhabala to fill sections later lost.
- A specific biography. "Charaka" functions as the redactor-figure in the tradition; biographical detail is scarce.
- "Discovering" germ theory, cellular biology, or other modern medical concepts. Classical Ayurvedic doctrine has its own explanatory frameworks, which are worth engaging with on their own terms.
