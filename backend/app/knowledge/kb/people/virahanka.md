## One-line identity

A Sanskrit prosodist widely credited by modern history of mathematics as the first to make the recurrence now known as the Fibonacci numbers *explicit* in the context of enumerating metrical patterns.

## Works

- Vrttajatisamuccaya (and related prosodic material preserved in the tradition)

## What they are cited for in this KB

- The explicit articulation of the recurrence *f(n) = f(n–1) + f(n–2)* in the prosodic context, several centuries before Leonardo Fibonacci
- An essential link in the attribution chain Pingala → Virahanka → Hemachandra — see the [Fibonacci / Virahanka attribution claim](../claims/fibonacci-virahanka-attribution.md)

## What they are NOT to be credited with

- "Discovering Fibonacci numbers" in the sense of using them outside prosody, or framing them as a sequence independent of the metrical enumeration problem
- A general theory of recurrences in the modern sense

## Sources on this person

- **Parmanand Singh's 1985 Historia Mathematica paper** — the primary modern scholarly treatment; essential reading for anyone writing about the "Indian origins of Fibonacci numbers"
