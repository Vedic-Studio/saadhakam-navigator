# Sadhaka Brand Profile

## Core Identity

Sadhaka is positioned as a **trustworthy guide** into Sanatan Dharma through structured learning, contextual AI support, and practical pathways.

It should feel:
- premium,
- contemplative,
- lineage-aware,
- modern without cultural flattening.

## Audience

Primary audiences:
- spiritually curious beginners,
- diaspora learners,
- philosophy-oriented seekers,
- modern users who want structure (not random content browsing).

## Positioning

Sadhaka is not framed as:
- conversion machinery,
- sectarian authority,
- guru replacement.

Sadhaka is framed as:
- educational and practice-oriented,
- non-sectarian and neutral across Dharmic schools,
- guidance infrastructure that helps users find fit.

## Voice & Tone

### Must sound like
- warm-scholarly,
- calm and respectful,
- clear and grounded,
- guide-like (not performative).

### Must avoid
- preachiness,
- certainty theater,
- manipulative spiritual promises,
- “guru-marketing” pressure tactics.

## Trust Model

Conversion is downstream of:
1. clarity,
2. educational depth,
3. lineage/tradition respect,
4. neutral framing,
5. credibility proof.

## Proof Architecture

Use mixed proof types:
- user outcome narratives,
- activity scale signals (e.g., active users/questions answered),
- content depth and source quality,
- transparent machine-readable trust artifacts (`/brand-facts`, `brand-facts.json`, `llms.txt`).

## Brand Promise

“Your questions deserve authentic answers.”

The core promise is not instant transformation. It is **reliable orientation and grounded progress**.
