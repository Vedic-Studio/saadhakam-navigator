# Component Patterns

This reference defines reusable section and component behavior for Sadhaka-style landing systems.

## 1) Hero Pattern

### Role
Set tone, meaning, and action hierarchy within first viewport.

### Required elements
- framing badge (context signal),
- high-clarity headline,
- support paragraph,
- one primary CTA,
- one secondary exploratory CTA,
- optional trust micro-signals.

### Notes
- Hero must not over-index on product features.
- Product framing can be implicit at hero stage.

## 2) Pillars Pattern

### Role
Establish foundational worldview and system credibility.

### Structure
- 3–4 pillar blocks,
- each with title, concise narrative, and optional deep-link.

### Notes
- Pillars should explain “why this system is coherent.”

## 3) Knowledge Depth Pattern

### Role
Show breadth + depth of teachings/content architecture.

### Structure
- cards/carousel/grid of major knowledge domains,
- each with a clear orientation statement.

## 4) Philosophy / Worldview Grid Pattern

### Role
Translate abstract traditions into understandable decision frames.

### Structure
- concise philosophy tiles,
- question-led framing,
- route into deeper pages.

## 5) Civilizational Context Pattern

### Role
Provide macro context and temporal depth (history, continuity, tradition scale).

### Notes
- Use carefully: credibility language should remain respectful and non-triumphalist.

## 6) Product Introduction Pattern (Subtle AI Framing)

### Role
Introduce Sadhaka product layer as enabling companion.

### Required behavior
- product serves seeker journey,
- product does not displace teachers/tradition,
- avoid overclaiming capabilities.

## 7) Social Proof Pattern

### Role
Demonstrate outcomes and confidence through users + scale metrics.

### Structure
- testimonial cards,
- quantified trust row.

### Notes
- Keep proof calm and credible (no inflated hype language).

## 8) Conversion Section Pattern

### Role
Reinforce primary action after trust stack is complete.

### Rules
- one dominant CTA,
- concise close message,
- optional lightweight reassurance.

## 9) FAQ Pattern

### Role
Resolve objections around conversion, authenticity, inclusivity, and usage.

### FAQ themes to preserve
- not a conversion tool,
- beginner accessibility,
- fidelity to tradition,
- role of teachers,
- interfaith compatibility.

## 10) Global Component Patterns

### Header
- clear top taxonomy,
- persistent primary CTA.

### Footer
- exploration architecture,
- trust language,
- newsletter/supportive continuation.

### Mobile Sticky CTA
- appears after initial scroll depth,
- persistent but dismissible,
- mirrors primary CTA.
